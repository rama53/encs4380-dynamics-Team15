Validation checks

How each check works

Plant K=1, τ=1 s, no saturation. (1) P only, Kp=9: simulate and measure the closed-loop time constant (time to 63.2% of the final value) and the steady-state error, then compare with τ/(1+KKp) and 1/(1+KKp). (2) PI with Kp=1, Ki=1: compare ωn=√(KKi/τ) and ζ=(1+KKp)/(2√(τKKi)) with the expected 1 and 1. (3) Several PI settings must all end with zero steady-state error for a step.
