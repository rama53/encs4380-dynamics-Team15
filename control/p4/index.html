P4: Tuning challenge

A random plant is hidden from you, like the sensors in Module 4. Tune Kp, Ki and Kd until the closed loop meets every part of the spec, then submit your gains for a score. The loop is stepped in time with RK4, the setpoint is r = 1, the control signal is limited to ±100, and anti-windup is on. u = Kp·e + Ki∫e dt + Kd·de/dt
